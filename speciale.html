Speciale Osteria

The Milanese classics, cooked the proper way, in a clean, contemporary venue.

Why I go

Speciale is a new-wave Milanese osteria in Isola, and it's packed most nights. The menu is rooted in tradition, mostly Lombard with some Piedmontese, Emilian and Roman dishes, but nothing about the place feels old.

The front room has cement-tile floors and green velvet booths. The main room keeps its original wooden floor, with handmade ceramic plates on the walls, scalloped lamps and a stained-glass ceiling over the open kitchen. The crowd is lively and mostly young. The service is relaxed but properly looked after.

Before you've ordered, bread arrives with whipped buffalo-milk butter and Coratina olive oil, which tells you how seriously they take the details. Come for dinner with friends or a date and order the classics.

What to order

  1. i.
    Mondeghili e maionese fatta in casaMilan's thrifty meatballs, traditionally made from leftover boiled meat, fried until crisp and served with house-made mayonnaise. Start here.
  2. ii.
    Vitello tonnatoThe Piedmontese classic: thin slices of cold veal under a creamy tuna and caper sauce. Light enough to share before the risotto.
  3. iii.
    Risotto alla milanese con pistilli di zafferano e ossobuco di vitelloThe reason to come: saffron risotto made with whole pistils, topped with a braised veal shank and gremolata. Don't leave the marrow.

Worth knowing

  • —Book ahead, especially for dinner: it's often full. Reservations are online, or you can phone.
  • —Vegetarians are well looked after: L'Orto, a section of the menu, is all vegetables.
  • —Save room for dessert: the tiramisù, crème caramel and tarte tatin are served at the table.
