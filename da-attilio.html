La Pizza dal 1964 – Da Attilio

Tall, soft, cheese-laden slices baked in the pan: the pizza Milan grew up on.

Why I go

Forget Naples for day: Milan has its own pizza. The trancio is baked in a pan, tall and soft in the middle with a crisp, oily base, and buried under mozzarella. For Milanesi it has simply always been what pizza means, as the neapolitan pizza only got more popular recently.

Da Attilio is a family-run pizzeria and the room is bright and modern rather than an old-school slice shop, and there are tables outside in the warmer months.

It's informal and great value: sit down, order a slice or two and a cold beer. Come hungry, because the slices are enormous and the toppings generous. After a week of risotto and cotoletta, it's the perfect change of pace.

What to order

  1. i.
    Margherita abbondanteThe benchmark: tomato and a thick blanket of mozzarella on that soft, pan-baked base. Start here to understand the style.
  2. ii.
    Prosciutto cotto e funghiSautéed mushrooms and folds of cooked ham piled onto the cheese: the classic Milanese slice.
  3. iii.
    Tonno e cipollaTuna and thinly sliced raw onion over the mozzarella, an old pizzeria favourite that works far better than it sounds.

Worth knowing

  • —Slices come in different sizes. The regular one is plenty for most people, so ask the staff before going large.
  • —Like other places mentioned here, its tough to get a seat at dinner peak times. Come before 19:30 if you can.
  • —Beyond the classics there's a long menu, including more modern 'contemporary' slices.
  • —There are tables outside in the warmer months.
