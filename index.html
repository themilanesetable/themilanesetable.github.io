Osteria · Isola

Speciale Osteria

Visit if you want the Milanese classics with a modern polish. Speciale is the newer wave of osteria: velvet booths, handmade ceramic plates on the walls and a lively, mostly young crowd that keeps it full most nights. The cooking stays rooted in tradition; the room just feels a little more of the moment than your typical trattoria.

Before you order

Eat like a Milanese

Risotto alla milanese

Saffron-gold risotto, traditionally enriched with beef marrow. Often served alongside ossobuco.

Cotoletta alla milanese

A bone-in veal chop, breaded and fried in butter until deep gold.

Ossobuco

Cross-cut veal shank braised slowly, finished with gremolata: lemon zest, garlic and parsley.

Mondeghili

Milanese meatballs, born as a way to use up leftover boiled meat, fried until crisp.

Cassoeula

A winter stew of pork cuts and savoy cabbage, usually eaten with polenta.

Panettone

Milan's tall, slow-risen sweet bread with candied peel and raisins. A Christmas institution.
