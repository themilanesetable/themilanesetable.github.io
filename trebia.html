Trattoria de la Trebia

A family-run grill trattoria where the steaks are aged in-house and cooked over embers in the middle of the room.

Why I go

La Trebia is a family-run grill trattoria a few minutes' walk from Porta Romana, the kind of place Milanesi book for business lunches, birthdays and long Saturday dinners alike.

The room is old-world: wood panelling, red-shaded chandeliers, draped curtains and a huge painting of an old Milanese city gate. In the middle of the main room is the fireplace where the meat is cooked over the embers. A separate wine room is lined with more than 700 labels.

They do a lot of the Milanese fundamentals but the grill is the reason to come. Steaks are aged in-house in a glass meat case, the pasta is handmade and the desserts are made on site. Come hungry, ideally with people who'll share a fiorentina.

What to order

  1. i.
    Fiorentina di ScottonaThe house flagship: a thick T-bone from Bavarian heifer, aged in-house and grilled over charcoal. It's sold by weight, so order one to share.
  2. ii.
    TagliataSliced grilled steak: all the flavour of the grill without committing to a whole fiorentina.
  3. iii.
    Costoletta di vitello alla milaneseThe bone-in veal cutlet, the third of the dishes the family calls its pride and joy.

Worth knowing

  • —Lunch Monday to Friday, dinner Monday to Saturday. Closed on Sunday.
  • —In winter, ask for a table near the fireplace.
  • —There's a private room for up to six, and the wine list mixes big names with good-value bottles.
