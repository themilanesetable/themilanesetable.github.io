Trattoria Masuelli

An old-school family trattoria where Lombardy meets Piedmont, and the risotto is the reason to go.

Why I go

Masuelli is a family-run trattoria in Porta Romana, and its menu sits happily between Lombardy and Piedmont. On one side are the Milanese classics, saffron risotto and the bone-in costoletta; on the other, Piedmontese dishes like agnolotti del plin. The cooking is hearty, generous and proudly traditional.

The room is old Milan at its best: wood panelling, a Gio Ponti chandelier, signed photos on the walls and crisp white tablecloths. It feels as if nothing has changed in decades, in the best possible way.

Come for a long lunch or a proper dinner, ideally with people who love the classics. The service is warm and old-school, and nothing about the place is in a hurry.

What to order

  1. i.
    Risotto alla milanese con ossobucoThe signature: saffron risotto made with whole pistils, topped with a slow-braised veal shank. This is the one to get, among the very best in MIlan.
  2. ii.
    Costoletta alla milaneseThe classic bone-in veal cutlet, fried until golden and finished with salt flakes.
  3. iii.
    Agnolotti del plinTiny pinched pasta parcels from the family's Piedmontese side of the menu. These arent part of the milanese tradition but they are really worth it.

Worth knowing

  • —Lunch is Wednesday to Sunday and dinner Monday to Saturday. It's closed on Sunday evening and at lunchtime on Monday and Tuesday.
  • —You can also check out the pasta e fagioli which is famously thick.
