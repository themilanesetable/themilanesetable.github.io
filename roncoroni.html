Roncoroni Classici Gastronomici

A tiny, deeply personal bistro cooking Italian and French classics, with the sauce at the heart of every plate.

Why I go

Roncoroni is a tiny bistro right by the Colonne di San Lorenzo: a counter and a couple of shared tables inside, around fifteen seats, plus a few more in the veranda.

It feels more like someone's home than a restaurant, with mismatched family tables, old coffee cups, shelves of cookbooks, manga and old food magazines, portraits of chefs on the wall and a hand-painted gold sign.

Don't expect the usual Milanese dishes: the 'classici' here are Italian and French gastronomic classics, like pâté en croûte, foie gras, cured meats, game and offal, with the sauces at the centre of everything. The menu changes with what comes in, and you're talked through it at the table.

What to order

  1. i.
    Pâté en croûteThe French classic: a rich meat terrine baked in a pastry crust. Good to share while you choose the rest.
  2. ii.
    Acciughe in salsa rossa o verdeAnchovies under a homemade red sauce or a sharp salsa verde, with good bread and mountain butter to mop up.
  3. iii.
    Quinto quarto del giornoThe offal dish of the day, always with a serious sauce. It changes constantly, so ask what's on.

Worth knowing

  • —Dinner only, Tuesday to Saturday from 18:30. Closed Sunday and Monday.
  • —The menu changes with the produce, so it looks a little different every time you go.
  • —Map apps show the building's address, but the entrance faces the Colonne di San Lorenzo.
